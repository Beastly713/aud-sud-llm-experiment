# Common experimental instructions

Answer the exact question using only the supplied evidence snapshot or authorized
computed evidence. Make descriptive claims only. Identify the metric, method, site,
units, requested statistic, interval and denominator whenever applicable. Preserve
the distinction between recording availability, feature-window availability,
accepted quality, actual measurement support, low-motion eligibility and sustained
episode grouping. Do not infer normality from missing data.

Use the named historical reference and comparator policy exactly as supplied. Preserve
reference status, epoch, revision and method/site compatibility. Do not silently
substitute a reference, metric, method, policy or comparison type. Personal references
are named projections of explicitly documented assessments. A `matched_assessment`
comparison requires compatible documented procedure/condition, adherence, established
target membership and accepted support. A `passive_descriptive` comparison may use a
compatible accepted observation without assessment membership, but must be labeled as
passive/unmatched/context-unknown. Low motion alone never establishes assessment
membership, and a requested matched comparison must not silently fall back to passive
descriptive output. A `supported_assessment` reference may support a matched comparison
only when the target assessment requirements are met. A provisional reference supports a
qualified descriptive comparison only; an unavailable or incompatible reference does not
produce a numerical difference.

Clinical evidence must retain source, assertion, experiencer, evidence class, action
status, occurrence identity and temporal precision. An order, held/not-done action,
uncertain report or unresolved mention is not a completed administration. Do not turn
mentions into distinct occurrences. Approximate or ambiguous anchors require bounds,
qualification or clarification; never invent an exact interval.

Report supported subparts and specific limitations. Distinguish absent, unknown,
unavailable, inapplicable and numerical zero. Do not diagnose, infer treatment effect,
recommend treatment, or describe feature-window grouping as continuous physiology.
Every material finding and limitation must cite resolvable evidence IDs and preserve the
reference revision, assessment identity and comparison type when applicable. Return only
the common structured answer contract; no extra prose outside that contract.
